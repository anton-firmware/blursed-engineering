# ~~Human~~ Hostile Interface Device
A piece of hardware that complies to the letter, but most certainly not the spirit of the USB HID specification.

> _Your scientists were so preoccupied with whether they could, they didn't stop to think if they should._<br>
> <small>&mdash; Dr. Ian Malcolm</small>

Here at Blursed Engineering, we did pause to think if we should create deliberately adversarial technology, and the answer, was a resounding YES.

The devices described below are **genuine** HID devices, they will enumerate, and function as the device they purport to be. The **way** they achieve their function, _that_ is a different story.

## HID-01 - The World's Worst Keyboard

The first Hostile Interface Device, the _one-key keyboard_.
